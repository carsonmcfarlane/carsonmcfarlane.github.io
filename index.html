Projects

Project: Custom Tooltip Refactor in AuctionFaster – Overcoming WoW Classic Client Changes (WotLK Era)

AuctionFaster is a performance-focused auction house and inventory addon for World of Warcraft Classic. In early 2023, the WotLK Classic client update (pre-patch ~February 2023) introduced silent, undocumented changes to tooltip behavior — including inconsistent fontstring population, delayed text loading in hidden tooltips, and stricter handling of :SetBagItem() calls. This broke legacy libraries like LibGratuity-3.0, which addons relied on to scan item tooltips for sellability (e.g., detecting soulbound, BoP, quest-bound, or conjured items). Inventory scans became unreliable, leading to nil errors, skipped items, or incorrect auction posting logic.

My Role & Changes

I refactored the Inventory module to eliminate dependency on the outdated LibGratuity-3.0 and implement a robust, self-contained tooltip scanner.
